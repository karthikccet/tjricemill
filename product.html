Sona Rice and Rajaboham Old Rice

TJ Rice proudly presents SONA RAJABOGAM OLD RICE, a premium quality product meticulously crafted to promote optimal health. Cultivated in the fertile lands of North Karnataka, this rice variety is renowned for its exceptional taste and aroma.

From aromatic pulao to delectable kheer, Sona rice is a versatile ingredient, ideal for both everyday meals and festive occasions. Our RAJABOGAM OLD RICE undergoes a unique aging process of up to 12 months, enhancing its qualities by reducing moisture content and intensifying its aroma, length, taste, and overall cooking performance.
