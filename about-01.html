Our About Us

Experience the taste of tradition with TJ rice.

Established in 1998, TJ Rice Mill has been a trusted name in Karur for over two decades. We are committed to delivering the finest quality TJ rice, sourced locally and milled to perfection. Our state-of-the-art facilities and traditional techniques ensure that every grain meets the highest standards. Experience the taste of tradition with TJ Rice Mill.

  • Premium TJ rice, finest quality, superior taste.
  • Traditionally milled, natural flavor, aroma preserved.
  • Hygienic processing, state-of-the-art, clean and safe.
